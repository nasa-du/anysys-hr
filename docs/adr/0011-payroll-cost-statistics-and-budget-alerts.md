# Payroll cost statistics and budget alerts

The cost architecture is a second organization dimension (company → cost center) that exists only to attribute payroll spend and to carry a monthly budget and a warning threshold. Payroll results already freeze the employee's cost center id, code, and name, but only the leaf: the full path is not frozen, while cost centers can be moved, renamed, or deleted after a month is calculated. The budget and warning threshold on `cost_center` are a single current configuration with no monthly history. M14 asks for a monthly report that rolls gross pay, employee social insurance, individual income tax, and net pay up through the cost-center tree, compares that spend with the monthly budget, returns a three-state warning, and drills down to the employees behind a row. The report must not reuse the department-summary aggregation basis, and must remain meaningful when payroll is unpaid, unconfirmed, stale, or when a cost center no longer exists.

We chose a **read-only, single-company, single-month cost report**. Its four amount columns are the fixed system fields `gross`, `social_this`, `tax`, and `net`; `paid_advance` is a payment-timing deduction and never participates. Budget consumption is measured on `gross` (应发工资): social insurance and individual income tax are employee withholdings already inside gross, not additional employer cost. Headcount is the number of distinct employees with a result in the selected data source, including zero and negative results, without FTE conversion; employees without a result are not counted. The company context isolates companies: the report never mixes companies or produces a cross-company total.

We chose to **freeze the full cost-center path on every payroll result** (and in every paid-snapshot employee entry): a root-to-leaf JSON array of `{id, code, name}` with no foreign key, extending the attribution snapshot of ADR-0006 and the frozen organization paths of ADR-0010. The path, not the current cost-center tree and not the employee's current `cost_org_id`, drives historical attribution. Moving, renaming, or deleting a cost center after calculation does not move or rename the spend in that month. An empty path is the **未分配** attribution: it is shown as a bottom row, its headcount and four amounts are included in the company total, and it is excluded from every cost-center budget, execution rate, and alert because it has no budget.

We chose **state-driven data sources**, matching the department-summary contract. A `paid` month reads the latest paid snapshot (`payroll_month_snapshot`); a `calculated` or `confirmed` month reads the current successful calculation run (`payroll_result` plus `payroll_result_value`); a `draft` month that still has old results reads those results and is marked stale; a month with no successful run returns an empty report plus its month status, never a zero-spend tree. An unlocked paid month is `confirmed` per ADR-0009: the report reads current results and states that a paid snapshot version exists. Freshness is non-blocking: `needs_recalc` (input, attendance, scope, or current-month configuration), a failed latest run, and `config_stale` appear as header badges on current-result views; paid snapshots never show recalculation badges. Calculated and confirmed months show their three-state budget status, but the header marks unconfirmed months as preview figures.

We chose **inclusive parent budgets**. The `budget` stored on a cost center covers that center's entire subtree; a child's budget is a breakdown reference, not an amount added to the parent. Every row compares its inclusive gross spend with its own budget, budgets are never summed across parent and child, and the company budget is the sum of root budgets only. If a root budget is zero while its subtree has spend, the company budget is marked as not covering that root. A zero budget means **未设预算**: no execution rate and no three-state warning. Otherwise the status is **超预算** when the execution rate is at least 100%, **接近预警** when it is at least `warn_pct`, and **正常** below that; exactly 100% is over budget, and a warning threshold of 100 never produces a near-warning state because the over-budget test wins. Comparisons use unrounded decimals; the rate is displayed to one decimal. Budgets and thresholds are current configuration and apply to every selected month; the page states this, and editing them changes historical execution rates. There is no monthly budget snapshot and no effective-date history in this phase.

We chose a **row set built from the current active cost-center tree plus the frozen paths of the selected data source**. Rows with results are positioned and named by their frozen path and are not duplicated at their current position; current active centers without results appear at their current position with zero and their current budget, so a budgeted center with no staff still reports 0% and 正常; inactive or deleted centers appear only when the month's frozen paths include them, marked as inactive or deleted; 未分配 is always last. A cost center created after the selected month therefore appears as a zero row in an older month, which is acceptable because the header states that the tree and budgets are current configuration while amounts are frozen at calculation. With no successful batch there is no tree and no zero status.

We chose a **self-contained drill-down and a payroll-list cost-center filter**. Clicking a row lists the employees in that center and its descendants for the same data source and month: employee number, name, administrative department, position, cost attribution, and the four amounts, ordered by employee number. Attribution always comes from the frozen path; paid snapshots take employee number and name from the snapshot, while current results join the employee record. The payroll list gains a cost-center filter including descendants, based on the same data source's frozen attribution rather than the employee's current cost-center assignment, plus an 未分配 option; a row links to the payroll list with company, month, and cost center preselected.

We chose **on-the-fly aggregation with no summary table**. Paid months aggregate the snapshot JSONB; current months join results with the four result values and an index on `(payroll_month_id, cost_org_code)`. The cost-center tree is small, so inclusive roll-up is computed in memory. The target is a p95 server response under two seconds for 1,000 employees in a month, with no N+1 queries. A materialized monthly summary table, or precomputed totals inside the paid snapshot, would add consistency and recalculation coupling for no benefit at this scale.

Alternatives rejected: aggregating by the current cost-center tree (rewrites history after a move or rename), freezing only the leaf and resolving ancestors from the current tree (cannot place a deleted center and still rewrites history), reading the employee's current cost-center assignment (a transfer rewrites every historical month), using the paid snapshot whenever one exists including after unlock (hides the current correction), offering separate official and current-result modes (a second view whose differences are already explained by the month status), own-level budgets that roll up (double counts when a parent amount is intended to cover its subtree), monthly budget snapshots or effective dates (budget is planning configuration, not a payroll ledger), a materialized cost-summary table (unnecessary at hundreds-to-1,000 employees), and multi-month trends, per-capita cost, cost-report export, budget approval, overspend notifications, cross-company consolidation, and cost reallocation in this phase.

## Consequences

- `payroll_result` and the paid-snapshot payload gain a frozen cost-center path; the existing leaf `cost_org_id`/`cost_org_code`/`cost_org_name` stay for filtering and display. DDL, routes, and error mapping belong to the API ticket.
- The payroll list gains a cost-center filter (including descendants, plus 未分配) that must use the same data source's frozen attribution as the cost report.
- Budget edits retroactively change historical execution rates; the report labels budgets as current configuration rather than freezing them per month.
- Cost-center moves, renames, and deletions never rewrite a calculated month's cost attribution; deleted centers with results appear as marked historical rows.
- Zero-spend active centers and budgeted centers with no staff remain visible, while inactive or deleted centers with no results stay hidden.
- The report never writes payroll results, paid snapshots, or basic-management data; unassigned spend is visible but never budgeted.
- Department summaries stay on the frozen department path and do not share the cost report's aggregation basis; the two reports are parallel views over the same results.
- Multi-month trends, per-capita cost, cost-report export, monthly budget history, budget approval, notifications, cross-company consolidation, and cost reallocation remain out of scope.
